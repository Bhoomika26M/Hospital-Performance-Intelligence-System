DATA INTERPRETATION NOTES
- Total Admissions counts admission records using Name; it is not a distinct-patient count.
- Medical Condition is not a department field.
- Patient Admissions and PMC Hospital Infrastructure are separate sources; do not imply a join without a verified common facility key.
- Total Hospital Beds represents reported capacity, not occupied or available beds.
- Average Monthly Patient Footfall is not a bed occupancy or utilization rate.
- Total Billing Amount is recorded billing, not necessarily cash collected or profit.

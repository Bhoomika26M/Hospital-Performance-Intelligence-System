WHAT THIS VIEW COVERS

Infrastructure in focus.

The Resource Utilization dashboard is based on the PMC Hospital Infrastructure dataset and is kept separate from the admissions analysis.

REPORTED CAPACITY

Total hospital beds

Sum of the reported number of beds by facility type. This is not a count of currently available beds.

FOOTFALL

Average monthly patient footfall

Displays the monthly patient footfall measure available in the infrastructure source.

FACILITY TYPE

Capacity by facility type

Compares reported bed counts and footfall across the facility categories provided.

DATA LIMITATION

No verified common hospital key

The two datasets are not joined. A reliable shared hospital identifier was not established, so infrastructure measures are presented independently.

SCOPE LIMITATION

No occupancy or equipment rate

The available data does not support calculating bed occupancy, real-time availability, or equipment utilization.

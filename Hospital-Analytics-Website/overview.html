Hospital Overview

A consolidated view of key measures from the patient admissions dataset.

01 04
WHAT THIS VIEW COVERS

The big picture, without the noise.

This dashboard brings together admission volume, average length of stay, billing amount, patient age, and emergency admissions.

01 / VOLUME

Total Admissions

Count of admission-date records. This is a record count, not a count of unique patients.

02 / DURATION

Average Length of Stay

Average of the recorded length-of-stay values, measured in days.

03 / FINANCIAL

Total Billing Amount

Sum of billing amounts as provided in the source data. Currency has not been confirmed.

PROJECT OVERVIEW

Hospital Operations & Patient Analytics

This project explores hospital admissions and infrastructure information through data preparation, KPI definition, Tableau dashboards, and a supporting website.

01 / DATA SOURCES

Two sources, analysed separately.

No common hospital identifier was verified, so the sources have not been joined.

SOURCE 01
⌁

Patient Admissions

Used for analysis of admission records, medical conditions, admission types, length of stay, age, and billing amounts.

  • Admission volume and monthly trends
  • Average length of stay
  • Condition and admission-type analysis
  • Billing amount comparisons
SOURCE 02
▦

PMC Hospital Infrastructure

Used for analysis of reported hospital infrastructure measures by facility type.

  • Reported number of beds
  • Facility-type comparisons
  • Monthly patient footfall measure
02 / KPI DEFINITIONS

What the measures mean.

Total Admissions

Count of admission-date records, not unique patients.

Average Length of Stay

Average of recorded length-of-stay values, in days.

Total Billing Amount

Sum of the billing amount field. The source currency has not been confirmed.

Emergency Admissions

Count of records where admission type is recorded as Emergency.

Total Hospital Beds

Sum of reported beds in the infrastructure dataset, not available or occupied beds.

Average Monthly Patient Footfall

A footfall measure from the infrastructure dataset. It should not be interpreted as occupancy.

03 / LIMITATIONS

What this project cannot claim.

These boundaries are part of the analysis, not footnotes to hide.

01 · No verified common hospital identifier

The datasets cannot be reliably joined with the available matching information.

02 · No patient identifier for readmission analysis

Readmission rates and unique-patient counts cannot be calculated from the available fields.

03 · Medical condition is not a department

Condition-level analysis should not be presented as department performance.

04 · Billing currency is unconfirmed

Billing values should not be assigned a currency symbol without source confirmation.

05 · Capacity is not utilization

Reported bed counts and footfall do not establish bed occupancy, bed availability, or equipment utilization.

A NOTE ON INTERPRETATION

Good analytics includes knowing where the data ends.

The dashboards describe the fields available in the supplied datasets. They are not a substitute for clinical judgment or operational systems with real-time information.
